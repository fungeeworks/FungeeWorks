About Fungeeworks

Rooted in fungi. Driven by useful innovation.

Fungeeworks Private Limited works across mushroom cultivation products, practical training and the exploration of fungal materials. We connect hands-on mushroom knowledge with ideas for using biological resources in new ways.

  • Mushroom spawn and grow-kit products
  • Training and practical learning opportunities
  • Research into mycelium-based materials
  • Exploring value from agricultural and mushroom residues
